From a million photos to the few you keep
The Library is Lightroom Classic's Library module, rebuilt so nothing waits on a disk. You choose a source, narrow it
with a query, fold the repeats, cull at a key press, and describe what's left. Each step works by key, by mouse and from
the command palette (DSN-23).
