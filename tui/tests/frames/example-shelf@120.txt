From a pasted link to a list people follow
Shelf keeps the albums, films, shows, books and things a person loves as rich cards, and lets them share a collection
with a few friends or publish it for anyone who trusts their taste. Saving a link works today; the library, publishing,
circles and share kits follow in phases 1 to 4, and the Atlas and Triage in the second release.
