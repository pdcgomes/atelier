Answered
Can collections hold other collections, and do saves file themselves? Collections nest one level, each keeping its own
audience, and auto-filing files saves into them: into private ones at once, into ones others see after the hour or as a
suggestion (docs/planning/10).
Is the inbox a queue to clear? Unsorted is a place: new saves start there and stay until a collection claims them, with
no badge or count to clear.
