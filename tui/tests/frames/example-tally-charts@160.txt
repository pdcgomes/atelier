• Three units, never mixed. Pounds for money paid or committed; share of a plan's      Boxes are regions, numbers point to the notes under each wireframe,

• Committed before variable. Every money chart splits subscriptions you're committed   where it stands:

• Steps, not slopes. Money lands on renewal days, so cumulative spend is drawn as      ✓ Built  In the app today, drawn so the new charts can be seen beside it.
  steps with each renewal labelled, and estimates as a range.
• Assumptions look like assumptions. Capacity that isn't measured yet is a band from
  half to twice, turning into a line once readings measure it.
• Colour carries meaning. Categories of spend, habits, kinds of suggestion and states
  of money each keep one colour on every chart and table; amber and red stay for what
  needs you, and a cell worse than your average gets a ▲ as well as its tint.
• Readable without hover. Labels are drawn, not hidden in tooltips; each chart carries
  its sentence and a Show as table behind it, and a chart with too little data waits
  rather than guessing.
