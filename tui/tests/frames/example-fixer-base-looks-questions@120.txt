Answered
Which Base Looks folders start open? A, as built: Favorites, Fixer and the folder holding the photo's look, that folder
opening again for each photo. (16 October)
How does the panel mark the photo's look in its folder? A, as built: its row highlighted, as History highlights the
current step. (16 October)
Where do Adobe's creative profile groups go? A, as built: one Creative folder holding a folder for each group. (16
October)
Does a search result show its folder? A, as built: its folder at the end of its row, in the quieter colour. (16 October)
Can Adobe's camera profile folders be hidden? A, for slice 3: Adobe Raw, Camera Matching and Creative hide as built-in
folders do; your own profiles don't. (16 October)
What can you do with an installed look no recipe uses? A, for slice 3: Remove from Base Looks, which lists it nowhere
and keeps its file for its edits; no Show in Finder. (16 October)
Does a colour look chosen after a monochrome one set Color? B: a colour look chosen in place of a monochrome one sets
Color, as Lightroom's colour profiles do, and Black & White chosen on a colour look stays; LookChoice does it wherever a
look is chosen. (16 October)
