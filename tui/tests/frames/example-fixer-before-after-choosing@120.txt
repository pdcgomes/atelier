Choosing the Before
Compare with Current is in the menu of each snapshot, of Original and of each History step, and the Before / After menu
and ⌘K list the same choices. Each sets the Before and shows the comparison, or changes the Before in place when it's
already showing.

CMP-14 also lets side by side be left and right or top and bottom by choice, rather than by the photo's shape; these
wireframes leave the layouts as they are.

What happens when
When                                               The Before
You choose a state while Before / After is hidden  It's set, and Before / After shows, in the layout question 1 settles
You choose one while it's showing                  It changes in place; the layout and the split's position stay
You apply a snapshot, undo, or go to another step  It stays; only the After changes
You delete its snapshot or clear History           It stays, with its name: it holds a copy of the settings
You crop, rotate or straighten                     It follows: the Before always takes the After's geometry, as it does…
You go to another photo                            It's kept or put back to Original, as question 3 settles
You choose Original                                It's back to what it is today
