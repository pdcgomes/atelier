The Lens Blur panel in Fixer's Develop and its tools on the photo, drawn from the design document of 14 October 2026 and the BLR-20 note. The feature was parked
by the owner on 14 October 2026, since it needs more research and calibration: its rows, BLR-20 and BLR-21 to BLR-28, are Deferred, with their phase set to
Later. These wireframes record the design for when it resumes, so every Lens Blur region is Later and the questions stay open; the refinement brushes (BLR-25)
will also wait on counsel's patent search (DSN-31). The parts of the app drawn around them, the Develop window, its other panels, the tool strip and the photo,
are Built.
