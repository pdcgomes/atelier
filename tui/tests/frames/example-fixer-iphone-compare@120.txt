What the counts say: for everyday edits the structure barely changes the count; it changes how much photo is left, how
much is learnt and how much is hidden. The concepts part ways on the less common adjustments, such as the fourth task.

What each prototype measures
The same four tasks in each, on the phone: time, taps and drags, wrong modules opened, accidental changes and where the
thumb lands, on an iPhone SE and the largest iPhone, with each hand, over three short sessions; and the strip at the
accessibility text sizes. For the compass, also the share of selections flicked without the ring, and each direction's
error rate.
