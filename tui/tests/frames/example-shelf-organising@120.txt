Organising
New saves start in Unsorted and file themselves into collections, which nest one level (docs/planning/10, after Kiroku
Station's threads). Nesting, Unsorted and filing by hand come with collections in Phase 2, rules in Phase 3, and learned
filing on Patron in the second release.
