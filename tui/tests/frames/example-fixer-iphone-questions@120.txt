Answered
Which concepts become prototypes? (CHC-44) All five, each to try: Bar and ruler, the Slider sheet, the photo as the
control, the Flick compass and My sliders (CHC-44, 13 October).
Eight modules in a fixed bar, with Optics inside Detail and Auto inside Looks? (CHC-45) Eight fixed modules, with Optics
inside Detail and Auto inside Looks (CHC-45, 13 October).
Where does the edit of a Photos photo live? (CHC-40) Both: the sidecar in Fixer's iCloud Drive container as the source
of truth, and a compact edit with a rendered version in Photos on Done (CHC-40, 13 October).
