What choosing a look applies
One choice in FixerRecipes, LookChoice, wherever a look is chosen (decisions 3, 6 and 7). A film stock sets its look,
Black & White when it's monochrome, and its eight grain, halation and bloom settings, those it doesn't list at their
defaults. A look that isn't a film, chosen over one, returns those eight to their defaults; chosen over a look that
isn't a film, it leaves them. A colour look chosen in place of a monochrome one sets Color, and Black & White you chose
on a colour look stays. Nothing else changes.
