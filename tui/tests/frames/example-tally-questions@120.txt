Answered
Do Neon and Inngest bill you directly, or on Vercel's invoice? Inngest through Vercel, Neon directly. Vercel's monthly
charge is split into lines for Vercel Pro and Inngest, the fixed plan at its price and the usage plan taking the rest;
Neon keeps its own card charge and merchant rule.
Which provider gets a connector next? IONOS, then Cloudflare; Render isn't worth one yet. IONOS does have a Domains API,
which gives each domain and its renewal date.
Budgets for groups or projects, as well as the month? Yes: a budget per group alongside the month's.
How many suggestions does the Overview show? Up to five, by money at stake, with the rest a click away.
Should Tally suggest a second Claude plan for side projects? Keep it, and go further: from the measured volume of work,
which changes, show the cheapest way to meet it, and what a cheaper option would cost in output.
Does undoing an inbox row also remove the rule it saved? Remove it, unless another row has matched it since.
How does a phone move between screens? A Menu pill at the bottom, as Bramble does it, opening the screens as tiles with
the theme and the account.
Ledger filters: which ones? Service and project, with each charge's share when a project is chosen.
