Answered
What icon does a look that isn't a film stock get? B, scene objects, with Golden Hour's low sun as the direction for now
(LKS-14). The existing film icons don't change. (13 October)
Where does each film stock live? Once, in the Recipes panel, with its film icon. Clicking applies the colour with grain,
halation and bloom; right-click › Apply Base Look Only. The Base Look menu and browser stop listing film stocks and
imported LUTs, showing one only while it's the photo's look. (13 October)
Where do Fixer's looks and the Film Styles live? On the right, in a short Base Look menu, and the Recipes panel's search
finds them too, listed after the recipes. (13 October)
Does hovering still preview? Yes, everywhere a look is listed. The browser and the Film Looks window both stay; a film's
colour alone previews while Apply Base Look Only is highlighted. (13 October)
Icons for more looks? A skill, fixer-look-icons, for more film stock icons and other kinds that convey a look's mood.
(13 October)
